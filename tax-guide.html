2 · 표준공제 — 왜 못 받나

한국 NRA는 표준공제($16,100, 2026)를 받을 수 없음. 강제로 항목별 공제(itemized)만 가능. 인도-미국 조약(Article 21(2))만 학생에게 표준공제를 허용 — 한국은 해당 조항 없음.

In English · Standard deduction & strategy

2b · Why no standard deduction — and what to do instead

Short answer: you cannot take the standard deduction while you are a nonresident alien (NRA), which is every year from 2025 through 2029. Two rules stack:

1. The tax code bars NRAs from the standard deduction. Form 1040-NR has no standard-deduction line — itemizing is the only way to reduce taxable income.  2. You're classified NRA because F1 students are "exempt individuals" whose days don't count toward the Substantial Presence Test for the first 5 calendar years. The only carve-out is the US–India treaty (Art. 21(2)); Korea has no such clause.

OPT doesn't change this — OPT is just work authorization under F1 status, so you stay an exempt individual inside the 5-year window. In 2030 you flip to resident alien: you gain the standard deduction but lose the FICA exemption.

What you CAN deduct — Schedule A (Form 1040-NR)

The NRA Schedule A is far narrower than the resident version. Realistic picture for you:

Itemized deductionReality for you
State & local income tax (SALT)Your big one — NY tax withheld (~$4,600/yr), capped at $10,000
Gifts to U.S. charitiesUS 501(c)(3) only; keep receipts
Casualty & theft lossesFederally-declared disaster areas only — rare
Other misc. deductionsMostly gutted by TCJA — likely $0
⚠ Always file Schedule A for the NY state tax (~$4,600). It drops federal taxable income from $94k → ~$89.4k, saving ~$1,000/yr. Skip it and you're taxed on the full $94k.

Above-the-line adjustments (not "itemized," but stack on top)

NRAs can also take these on Schedule 1, in addition to itemizing: student loan interest (up to $2,500, qualifying US loans) · HSA contributions (if EY offers an HSA-eligible plan) · Traditional IRA (if eligible) · US–Korea treaty exclusions (Art. 21 student item — excludes income rather than deducting; confirm with EY's provider).

Strategies — your real lever is pre-tax, not deductions

Because you're denied the $16,100 standard deduction, the biggest tax savings come from shrinking taxable income before it's taxed. Every pre-tax dollar saves you ~28% (your marginal rate: 22% federal + 6% NY).

S1
Max your 401(k). Pre-tax contributions cut both federal and NY taxable income — your single most powerful move as an NRA. ~$0.28 saved per $1. Bonus: EY match once eligible.
S2
Take every pre-tax payroll option. HSA (if offered — triple tax-advantaged), FSA, and pre-tax commuter benefits ($340/mo cap, 2026). These reduce taxable income the standard deduction would have — your workaround for not getting it.
S3
Never skip Schedule A. Always itemize the NY state tax (~$4,600) — it's free money left on the table otherwise (~$1,000/yr).
S4
Claim student-loan interest (up to $2,500) above-the-line if you have qualifying US loans — stacks on top of itemizing.
S5
File W-8BEN with your broker → 15% Korea-treaty dividend withholding instead of the default 30%.
S6
Don't day-trade US stocks until 2030 — the 30% NRA capital-gains flat rate can apply while you're a nonresident.
S7
Bunch charitable gifts into one year if you ever give large amounts, since SALT alone may be your only other itemized line.
S8
Plan the 2030 dual-status year. That's when you become a resident alien (gain the standard deduction, lose FICA exemption). Timing big deductions/income around the switch can matter — ask a CPA.
S9
File with Sprintax, not TurboTax. TurboTax forces the resident 1040 and breaks your NRA status. EY likely provides free tax prep for new hires — use it.
Sources: IRS Instructions for Form 1040-NR · About Form 1040-NR

3 · NY 주세 (비거주) & NJ 주세 (거주)

NY에서 일하므로 NY 비거주 신고(IT-203). NJ 거주자이므로 NJ-1040도 신고하지만, NJ가 NY 납부분을 크레딧으로 빼주어 실질 NJ 납부는 ≈ $0.

NY는 $8,000 표준공제 허용 → NY 세금이 "겨우" ~$4,600/년. NJ 세금은 NY 크레딧으로 거의 전액 상쇄 (NY세 ≈ NJ에서 냈을 세금).
⚠ NJ 거주가 핵심. NYC로 이사하면 NYC 거주세(~$2,950/년) + 보통 더 높은 월세까지 추가. 이사 전 반드시 계산.

4 · 투자 시 함정 (NRA 특이사항)

양도세 30% 함정 (검증 필요): 미국 체류 183일 이상인 NRA의 미국 원천 양도차익은 15%가 아니라 30% 단일세율이 적용될 수 있음 (IRC §871(a)(2)). F1 "면제 개인"의 일수 산정은 논쟁 여지가 있어, 거주외국인(2030) 되기 전엔 미국 주식 데이트레이딩 자제. 실제 적용은 세무 전문가 확인.
배당 원천징수: 미국 원천 배당은 한국-미국 조약으로 15% 자동 원천징수 (W-8BEN 제출 시; 미제출 시 기본 30%). 증권사(Fidelity 등)가 자동 처리.
장기 양도세 우대(시민 기준): 2026년 싱글 15% 구간은 ~$49,450 초과부터. NRA에겐 위 30% 함정이 우선할 수 있음.
